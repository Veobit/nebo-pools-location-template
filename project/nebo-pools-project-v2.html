Project Overview

A resort-style sanctuary for a growing family

The Morrison family approached us with a clear vision: transform their expansive Oak Ridges backyard into a private resort that would serve as the centerpiece for family gatherings and summer entertaining for generations to come.

The property presented unique challenges—sitting on York Region's characteristic clay soils with a gentle slope toward the ravine. Our team worked closely with TRCA to ensure full compliance while delivering a design that maximized the stunning views.

The result is a 16' × 36' freeform fiberglass pool with integrated spa, natural stone decking, and a custom water feature that creates the gentle sound of flowing water throughout the space.

Services & Features

Premium Fiberglass Shell

16' × 36' freeform design with integrated tanning ledge and bench seating. 25-year structural warranty.

Integrated Spa

Spillover spa with hydrotherapy jets and LED chromotherapy lighting for year-round relaxation.

Natural Stone Deck

800 sq ft of premium travertine decking with non-slip finish, seamlessly integrated with landscape.

Smart Automation

Pentair IntelliCenter system with app control for lighting, temperature, and water chemistry.

Complete Permits

Full coordination with City of Richmond Hill and TRCA. All inspections passed first time.

Water Feature
